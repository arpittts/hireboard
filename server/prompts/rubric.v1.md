# Interview answer rubric (v1)

Each answer is scored from 1 to 4 on four criteria. A follow-up answer counts as part of the same answer.

## Relevance: does it answer what was asked?
- **4** Answers the exact question directly and stays on it throughout.
- **3** Answers the question, with some drifting or a partly different example.
- **2** Only loosely related, or answers an easier question than the one asked.
- **1** Off-topic, a non-answer, or a refusal.

## Specificity: concrete details about the candidate's own work?
- **4** A real situation with the candidate's own actions ("I…") and concrete details: numbers, tools, names, timelines.
- **3** A real situation with some details, but the candidate's own part or the result is a bit vague.
- **2** Mostly general statements ("we usually…", "I would…") with one or two details.
- **1** Entirely generic; could be anyone's answer.

## Structure: easy to follow?
- **4** Clear order: for a story, situation → task → action → result (STAR); for a technical question, a clear approach step by step. Ends with a result or conclusion.
- **3** Mostly clear, with one missing or muddled part (for example no result).
- **2** Hard to follow: jumps around, or key parts are missing.
- **1** No discernible structure, or too short to have one.

## Depth: shows understanding beyond the surface?
- **4** Explains why: trade-offs, alternatives considered, what was learned or would be done differently.
- **3** Some reasoning beyond what was done, but brief.
- **2** Says what was done with little or no reasoning.
- **1** No reasoning at all.
